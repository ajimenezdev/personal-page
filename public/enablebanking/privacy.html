1. What this application is

Kalia is a private, non-commercial tool used by the account holder himself to view his own bank account balances and transactions in one place. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. What data is accessed

Through Enable Banking, and only with the explicit consent of the account holder given at his bank, the application retrieves read-only account information: account identifiers, balances and transactions of the accounts the account holder chooses to link. No payment can be initiated with this application.

3. How the data is processed

The data is processed and stored solely on the account holder's own equipment. It is used only to produce personal financial summaries for the account holder. The data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with third parties, other than Enable Banking acting as the technical conduit for the bank connection.

4. Retention

The data is kept locally only for as long as it is useful to the account holder and can be deleted at any time. Consent for the bank connection can be withdrawn at any moment via the bank or Enable Banking, after which no further data is retrieved.

5. Rights

The account holder may request access to, rectification or erasure of his data at any time by writing to the contact email above.
